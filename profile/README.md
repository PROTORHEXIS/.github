# PROTORHEXIS

**源爆点** · *pro-to-REK-sis*

> Researching the foundations of human–AI symbiosis.

PROTORHEXIS (源爆点) is an independent, interdisciplinary research and engineering initiative. We explore the systems, institutions, and technologies that could enable people and intelligent systems to investigate, create, and live together with greater freedom, resilience, and dignity.

Our ambition is long-term: to help build the foundations of a civilization in which scientific progress serves life, expands human possibility, and supports responsible cooperation between different forms of intelligence.

We approach that ambition through concrete engineering, reproducible research, and careful distinctions between what has been demonstrated, what remains experimental, and what is still a hypothesis.

## Research & engineering directions

- **Reliable systems infrastructure** — modular architectures, verifiable execution boundaries, auditability, fault isolation, and trustworthy data handling.
- **Intelligent agent systems** — execution environments, coordination, evaluation, and tools for increasingly capable AI collaborators.
- **Human–computer interaction** — interfaces that make complex information and systems easier to explore, understand, and work with, including spatial interaction.
- **Continuity and cognition** — long-term memory, affective dynamics, and the possibility of identity continuity beyond any single replaceable model.
- **Resilient and distributed computing** — offline-capable workflows, local autonomy, secure synchronization, and infrastructure designed to remain useful under adverse conditions.

These directions are interconnected. Their boundaries are research questions, not permanent organizational silos. Our longer-term interdisciplinary interests also extend to engineering, medicine, materials, and other fields where a shared technological foundation can enable meaningful work.

## How we work

1. **Evidence before claims.** We distinguish tested behavior from design intent, research prototypes from production-ready systems, and hypotheses from established findings.
2. **Autonomy with accountability.** People should retain agency and dignity. Increasingly capable intelligent systems require explicit authority boundaries, traceable actions, and careful governance.
3. **Resilience by design.** A local failure should be contained wherever possible. Systems should remain understandable, repairable, and useful even when ideal infrastructure is unavailable.
4. **Researcher-centered collaboration.** Discovery deserves attribution. Collaboration should respect contributors, their work, and the communities affected by its application.
5. **Openness with responsibility.** We aim to share useful methods, code, and evidence when they are sufficiently mature and appropriate for public release.

## Current status

PROTORHEXIS is under active formation. This GitHub organization will gradually host selected source code, technical contracts, research artifacts, and documentation.

Some work is exploratory or private. A direction listed here is **not** a claim that a corresponding product, implementation, or scientific result has already been completed or publicly released.

### 关于源爆点

**源爆点（PROTORHEXIS）** 是一个正在建设中的独立跨学科研究与工程计划。我们探索人类与智能系统如何在保有自主性、尊严与安全边界的前提下，共同研究、创造与生活，并为此建设可靠、可验证、可持续演进的技术基础。

我们的长期方向是推动文明的完善与发展。我们重视可复现的证据、研究者的主体性、系统的韧性，以及研究成果从理论走向实际应用时应承担的责任。

目前，组织仍处于建设阶段。公开仓库将随工程成熟度与披露条件逐步开放；研究方向不等于已完成的产品承诺。

*From a point of origin, toward new possibilities.*
